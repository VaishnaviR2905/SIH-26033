## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1.DETAILED EXPLANATION OF THE PROPOSED SOLUTION
    We propose to develop a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers. Farmers can list their products, set prices, and receive orders through the platform. It also provides online payments, transportation support, and AI-based price prediction to improve agriculture trading.
2.HOW IT ADDRESSES THE PROBLEM 
    Our platform reduces the dependency on multiple middlemen by connecting farmers directly with buyers. This helps farmers earn better profits and allows consumers to purchase fresh agricultural products at lower prices. Transparent pricing and delivery support make the buying and selling process easier and more reliable.
 3.INNOVATIONS AND UNIQUENESS
    Our solution combines direct selling, AI based price prediction, demand forecasting, and logistics support in one platform. It provides a simple and user friendly system that helps farmers make better selling decisions, reduces unnecessary costs, and creates a fair agricultural marketplaces.
 
## Technical Approach
1.TECHNOLOGIES TO BE USED
   The proposed solution, Smart Farmer Marketplace, uses modern technologies to connect farmers directly with consumers. HTML, CSS and JavaScript are used to develop an attractive and user-friendly website. Python (Django) is used for backend development to manage farmer registration, product details and customer orders. MySQL is used as the database to store farmer information, product prices, quantities and order details. Smartphones and laptops allow farmers and consumers to access the platform easily. An internet connection helps users communicate and place orders online. Online payment facilities can also be included to make transactions easier and faster. These technologies help create a simple, secure and efficient marketplace.
2.METHEDOLOGY AND PROCESS FOR IMPLEMENTATION
  The implementation process starts with farmer and consumer registration on the online platform. After registration, farmers can upload their agricultural products along with product names, quantities, prices and images. Consumers can browse the available products and compare prices before purchasing. They can select the required products and place orders directly with farmers. The farmer receives the order details and confirms the purchase. The products are then packed and delivered to the consumers. The platform also provides order details and tracking facilities to make the process easier. By connecting farmers and consumers directly, the system reduces the need for multiple intermediaries. This helps farmers receive better earnings while consumers get fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.

 ##Research and References
   Agricultural marketing plays an important role in connecting farmers with consumers. However, multiple intermediaries increase marketing costs and reduce farmers' earnings. Research by Chand et al. found that farmers received only about 25% of the consumer price for carrots and 41% for tomatoes in the studied markets. Another study by G. R. Chintala highlighted the importance of direct marketing in reducing unnecessary intermediaries. Research by Aparajita Goyal showed that better market information and alternative selling channels helped farmers receive better prices. The Government of India introduced the e-NAM platform to improve agricultural trading and price transparency. These findings show the need for an efficient marketing system. A direct online marketplace can help farmers reach more customers and improve their earnings. It also allows consumers to purchase fresh products at reasonable prices. Therefore, the proposed Smart Farmer Marketplace provides a better connection between farmers and consumers.
